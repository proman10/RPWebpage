01 / The digital witness

A WATCH CAN REMEMBER.
BUT CAN IT BE TRUSTED?

Wearable devices can record movement, time and physiological signals that may support forensic event reconstruction. However, recorded telemetry is not automatically trustworthy and should be validated before its evidentiary reliability is assessed.

03 / The core system

THE DVTF FRAMEWORK

From raw telemetry to structured forensic evaluation.

01

ACQUIRE & PRESERVE

Lawful, documented acquisition of wearable telemetry using forensically sound extraction protocols.

02

SIGNAL QUALITY

PPG + IMU signal-quality assessment before interpretation or cross-modal comparison.

03

VERIFY

Temporal normalization plus cross-modal correlation of independent telemetry streams.

04

EVALUATE

Anomaly detection and structured confidence assignment based on convergence.
